Why Lunisolar?

📅

Perfectly Regular

Every month has exactly 28 days and 4 complete weeks. The 1st is always Solday. The 15th is always Solday. Always.

🧠

Easy to Remember

Thirteen months, simple Latin names: Monoluna (1st), Diluna (2nd), Triluna (3rd), and so on. The name tells you the number.

🔮

Plan Ahead Forever

Your birthday in 2050? Same weekday as this year. Holidays, anniversaries, schedules - they never shift around.

How It Works

The Lunisolar calendar follows the natural rhythm of the moon - 13 months of 28 days, plus one or two extra days at year's end.
